BEFORE YOU START

A few practical details.

Do I need Java or a diagram server?

No. Aliot includes its language service and the OMG standard library. You do not need Java or PlantUML.

Does it work in the browser?

Language services and editable diagrams work in vscode.dev and github.dev. Archive and abstract-syntax interchange operations, and the companion CLIs, require a desktop environment.

Is an AI account required?

No. AI tools are optional and need a compatible, separately configured VS Code agent. Aliot includes no AI model or credentials. A chosen agent service handles shared context under its own policies.

Where does my model live?

Your .sysml and .kerml files stay in the workspace. Diagram layouts live under .vscode/sysml/diagrams/. Aliot collects no telemetry. You choose what to share or commit.

Which theme should I use?

Choose Aliot Dark for a dark editor, or SysML v2 Gray for a light gray editor. Diagrams also follow your active VS Code theme.

Is Aliot free?

Aliot is available under its Freeware License. Bundled libraries retain their upstream licenses. The project is under active development; small reproductions make useful bug reports.
