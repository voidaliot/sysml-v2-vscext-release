### Added

- **An individual is one identified thing.** Hover an `individual` usage and it names the definition that carries the identity and states the multiplicity `[1]` it has without writing one. The diagram Properties pane and the abstract-syntax export say the same.
- **Three checks guard identity.** SSM040 reports `individual` on something with no life to identify (quick fix removes the keyword), SSM041 a usage typed by two individual definitions, and SSM042 an `individual` usage that names none.
- **Identity is found wherever it is written.** Through an alias, from the occurrence that owns a timeslice or snapshot, and behind a `#Tag` or an `end` keyword.

### Fixed

- Hover states a connector end's own multiplicity, such as `end [0..*] occurrence e : V1`, instead of the default [1].
