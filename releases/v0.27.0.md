### Added

- **Performed actions read as text.** A part lists its `perform` claims in a `perform actions` compartment, and an action lists the parts that perform it under `performed by`.

### Changed

- **Deeply nested action flows stay readable.** Nesting stops after eight action frames. A deeper action is drawn as a box that still opens its own flow.

### Fixed

- **Action flows no longer list what they draw.** A succession or flow drawn as an arrow is not repeated as a compartment row, as in state diagrams. One the view cannot draw keeps its row.
