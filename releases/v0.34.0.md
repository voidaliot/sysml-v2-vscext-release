### Added

- **Modifiers out of order are explained.** A declaration prefix written in a non canonical order now raises SYN025, naming the word that is out of place, with a quick fix that rewrites the whole prefix.
- **Format Document writes the canonical modifier order.** Formatting puts the leading modifiers in the order the specification gives them, whatever the style options say.

### Fixed

- **A modifier no longer splits its declaration.** Formatting kept `derived ref attribute a;` on one line instead of breaking after the modifiers.
- **A closing brace lines up with its opener.** Formatting wrote every `}` at its members' indentation, one level too deep.
